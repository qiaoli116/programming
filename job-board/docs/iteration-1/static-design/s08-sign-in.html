Design notes

Alternative state · UC-08 1 – sent here from “Apply” while signed out
Please sign in to apply for Frontend Developer at Northwind Digital. After signing in you will go straight to the application form (S-03).
UC-09 step 3 – after Sign out
You have been signed out.
Alternative state · UC-08 4a – wrong email or password
Email or password is incorrect.
Alternative state · UC-08 3a – a field is empty
Enter your email address.
Enter your password.
  • One screen serves both kinds of user. After signing in a seeker goes back to where they were heading (or S-01), and an employer goes to S-05.
  • The error message does not say whether the email or the password was wrong, so it cannot be used to find out which emails have accounts.
  • There is no “Register” or “Forgot password” link: accounts are pre-created in the prototype, see decision 15.
  • The Demo accounts box exists only for the class demo and is removed in the production iteration.
  • Sign out has no screen of its own. It is a button in the header (visible on every signed-in screen) that ends the session and shows this page with the confirmation above.
