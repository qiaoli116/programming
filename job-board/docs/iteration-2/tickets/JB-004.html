The AI sometimes adds generic criteria (for example the cover letter) that every job has

1. Report

The AI is asked to derive up to five criteria from the posting itself. For some jobs it spends one of the five on something every posting has, such as "includes a cover letter" or "applies through the site". Such a criterion says nothing about whether the seeker fits the role, uses up one of the five places, and, depending on the answer, adds a 10 or a 0 to the average.

The answers are valid, so validation does not reject them. It is a weakness of the prompt.

Done when: the prompt tells the AI to leave out criteria that apply to every job, a live check on the same jobs no longer shows them, and the prompt text is recorded in the devlog.

2. Investigation

QuestionAnswer
Where is the prompt?server/src/scoring/ (buildPrompt). It is a single function, tested for what it contains.
Is the data wrong?No. The seeded postings do mention applying with a cover letter (the closing sentence added to each job), so the AI is reading real text; it is just not a useful criterion.
Why was it not caught by tests?The fake AI never writes such criteria. Only reading real answers shows it.

3. Solution (proposed, not built)

Add one instruction to the prompt: only use requirements that describe the person or the work; ignore how to apply, the company's benefits and standard paperwork. Optionally, cut the shared closing sentence from the seeded descriptions so the demo data does not invite it.
