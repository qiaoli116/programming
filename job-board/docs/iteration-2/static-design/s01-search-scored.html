Sign in to score your results against your profile.
  • Scoring is opt-in: the list first appears exactly as in iteration 1, then the seeker chooses "Score these results".
  • The overall score is a number out of 10: the average of the criteria (Meets 1, Partly 0.5, Does not meet 0, Unknown ignored). Green 7 and above, amber 4 to 7, red below 4.
  • "Sort by match" appears only after scoring. Jobs with no score sort last; ties keep the keyword order.
  • Scoring never removes a job and never blocks Apply.
  • The disclaimer sits beside the button as well as on the profile screen.
